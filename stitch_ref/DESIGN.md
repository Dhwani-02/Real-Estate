## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end architectural luxury, channeling the precise moment twilight converges with structural brilliance. Built for discerning buyers, architectural collectors, and ultra-high-net-worth individuals, the visual identity balances monumental scale with intimate refinement. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Editorial Modernism with Ambient Architectural Lighting**:
- **Monolithic Compositions:** Expansive negative space, razor-sharp alignments, and structured layouts evoke the experience of standing within modern architectural marvels.
- **Cinematic Atmosphere:** Rich twilight slate and nocturnal depth paired with radiant amber luminance mimic curvilinear facade fins backlit during the golden hour.
- **Controlled Restraint:** Every decorative element is stripped unless it serves an architectural purpose. Luxury is signaled through extreme precision, deliberate pacing, and bespoke proportions rather than ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from dusk architecture: twilight sky transitions, deep metallic louvers, and interior warm luminescence.

- **Primary (`#E5A96A`):** Glowing architectural amber. Used for primary calls to action, accent flourishes, active indicators, and high-prestige milestones.
- **Secondary (`#D48B47`):** Burnished bronze. Provides depth to interactive states, hovered elements, borders on focus, and tertiary badging.
- **Tertiary (`#22314E`):** Dusk navy tone. Serves as structural surface layering, subtle structural outlines, and elevated container backgrounds.
- **Neutral (`#0B111E`):** Deep twilight slate. The foundational canvas that establishes the signature dark nocturnal baseline.
- **Supporting Accents:** Crisp archival white (`#F8F9FA`) and muted warm stone (`#A4B3C6`) provide legible body typography and balanced contrast across all elevated surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the dramatic editorial stature of Playfair Display with the clean geometric restraint of Manrope.

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display delivers an authoritative editorial tone reminiscent of luxury architecture publications. Large titles embrace generous vertical line height and slight negative letter spacing to preserve optical harmony.
- **Body:** Manrope set in lighter weights (`300` and `400`) ensures crystalline legibility over dark slate backdrops without overpowering photography.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase settings with extended tracking (`0.08em` to `0.18em`) bring architectural drafting precision to metadata, property statistics, and navigation indices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors architectural floor plans: unhurried, proportional, and deliberate.

- **Grid Framework:** A 12-column fluid grid on desktop transitioning to a 6-column grid on tablet and 4-column grid on mobile devices.
- **Horizontal Margins:** Expansive outer boundaries (`4rem` on desktop) isolate property showcases, allowing imagery to command full visual attention.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical section offsets (`space-xl` and above) prevent visual clutter, establishing an exhibition-like pacing between residence portfolios, floor plans, and architectural narratives.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses architectural illumination and tonal layering rather than blunt drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation is primarily communicated through stepped surface luminance. Canvas depth moves from `#0B111E` (base ground) to `#121A2A` (elevated panels) up to `#1C283E` (floating dialogs and overlays).
- **Subtle Amber Luminescence:** Higher-tier interactive components cast an ultra-soft, low-opacity warm amber glow (`rgba(229, 169, 106, 0.08)` to `rgba(229, 169, 106, 0.16)` with 24px–48px blur radius), recreating the warm architectural backlighting of evening dusk.
- **Hairline Ghost Borders:** Structural panels, glass overlays, and image containers use 1px border strokes with muted dusk slate (`rgba(164, 179, 198, 0.12)`) to delineate edges cleanly against deep twilight grounds.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, nearly monolithic posture with soft edges (`roundedness: 1`). 

- **Edge Philosophy:** Sharp right angles tempered with subtle 4px corner softening preserve modern architectural discipline while avoiding harsh computer-generated rigidity.
- **Architectural Curvature:** While UI elements (buttons, inputs, cards) remain disciplined and rectangular, full-bleed media treatments and hero layout masks can borrow sweeping curvilinear paths echoing structural facades and organic cantilevers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid glowing amber background (`#E5A96A`) with deep slate text (`#0B111E`), uppercase label styling (`label-md`), and tight 4px border radiuses. Hover state deepens to burnished bronze (`#D48B47`) accompanied by a subtle ambient golden wash.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent fill with a 1px border (`rgba(229, 169, 106, 0.4)`) and warm white text. Hover state shifts to a tinted dusk navy background with crisp white typography.
- **Text Link:** Understated uppercase typography paired with an animated 1px hairline underline that expands outward from the center.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Container:** Grounded deep slate fill with a 1px perimeter border (`rgba(164, 179, 198, 0.18)`).
- **Active State:** Hairline switches to vibrant amber (`#E5A96A`) with an ambient 4px perimeter glow.
- **Typography:** Placeholder text rendered in muted warm stone; entered text remains pure white.

### Cards & Residence Showcases
- **Structure:** Edge-to-edge photography complemented by a bottom-anchored content pad. Background transitions subtly from transparent to `#121A2A`.
- **Borders:** Thin 1px border (`rgba(164, 179, 198, 0.1)`) that intensifies to `#D48B47` upon cursor hover.
- **Metadata Display:** Property specifications (square footage, bedrooms, price) set in uppercase tracked sans-serif separated by subtle bronze vertical dividers.

### Chips & Spec Badges
- **Display:** Compact rectangular badges with 4px corner radiuses.
- **Visuals:** Semitransparent midnight navy backing (`rgba(34, 49, 78, 0.6)`) with high-contrast amber or warm white text, framed by a 1px hairline stroke.

### Floor Plan & Gallery Viewers
- **Interactive Layers:** Custom zoom and navigation docks hovering with backdrop blur (`blur(16px)`), framed in translucent slate (`rgba(18, 26, 42, 0.85)`), and accented with amber crosshairs.
